This repository contains independently cleaned water quality datasets from the 2011, 2016, and 2021 National Wetland Condition Assessment (NWCA) survey cycles, along with a harmonized dataset combining observations across all three years. The individual survey datasets, identified by their respective survey years, contain the cleaned records and retained supporting variables from each assessment. The 2011 dataset contains 682 observations, the 2016 dataset contains 739 observations, and the 2021 dataset contains 724 observations. These files preserve survey-specific information and are intended to support analyses within individual assessment years, as well as verification of the data preparation procedures.

The combined harmonized dataset contains 2,145 observations across the three survey cycles, with standardized variable names and measurement units to facilitate cross-year comparisons. Its 20 variables include the survey year, unique and site identifiers, standardized wetland classification, ammonia and nitrate/nitrite concentrations and associated analytical status classifications, laboratory quality indicators, method detection limits, reporting limits, total nitrogen, total phosphorus, pH, conductivity, survey weights, and survey eligibility. Laboratory detection and reporting limits unavailable for particular survey years or observations are represented as missing rather than estimated. The harmonized dataset is intended to provide a consistent analytical structure while preserving information necessary to evaluate differences in laboratory reporting practices and measurement uncertainty.

Separate data-cleaning logs accompany the datasets and document the procedures applied during data preparation. Each log records the variable or group of variables evaluated, the identified issue, the action taken, the methodological justification, and the resulting impact on the dataset. These records include assessments of missing observations, unit conversions, variable standardization, laboratory quality classifications, censored measurements, and quality-control results. The logs are provided to facilitate independent review, methodological transparency, and reproducibility of the data-cleaning process.

The cleaned survey datasets and harmonized dataset serve complementary purposes. The individual survey files retain information relevant to their respective assessment years, whereas the combined dataset provides a standardized structure for analyses spanning multiple survey cycles. Researchers should consult the associated cleaning logs and original EPA documentation when evaluating laboratory qualifications, survey-specific reporting conventions, and the suitability of individual variables for statistical analysis.
